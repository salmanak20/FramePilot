FramePilot

Desktop app · Windows · macOS · Linux

Make short clips.
Approve every post.

FramePilot turns long videos and live streams into captioned vertical clips for Reels, Shorts and TikTok. It runs on your computer in its own app window; no browser or FramePilot account is needed.

Download the latest release

Install FramePilot

WINDOWS 10/11
  1. Download and run FramePilot-Setup-windows-x64.exe.
  2. If SmartScreen appears, choose More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Open FramePilot from your desktop or Start menu.
MACOS
  1. Download the Intel or Apple Silicon ZIP for your Mac.
  2. Open it and drag FramePilot.app to Applications.
  3. For the first launch, Control-click the app and choose Open.
LINUX X64
  1. Install GTK and WebKitGTK: sudo apt install libgtk-3-0 libwebkit2gtk-4.1-0.
  2. Download and extract FramePilot-linux-x64.tar.gz.
  3. Run ./FramePilot/FramePilot.

The first launch downloads the speech model (about 480 MB). Keep an internet connection available until setup finishes. FFmpeg is included in the desktop builds.

Make your first clips

  1. Open FramePilot and paste a YouTube, Twitch or Kick link, or upload a video file.
  2. Choose Make clips. FramePilot finds moments, adds captions and prepares vertical videos.
  3. Open a clip to adjust its title, text, timing or caption style.

Video processing and clip rendering run on your computer. If you add an AI key, the transcript is sent to the AI provider you select.

Set up AI (optional)

  1. Go to Google AI Studio and create an API key.
  2. In FramePilot, open Settings → AI, choose Gemini, paste the key, then choose Save and test.

Without an AI key, clips are still created using speech and audio levels.

Approve and publish

Connect YouTube, Instagram or TikTok in Settings → Connected apps. Choose an account for a creator, review the clip, and select Approve & publish or Approve & schedule. FramePilot never publishes a newly created clip without your approval.

To use n8n, enter your production webhook URL in the same settings page. FramePilot sends the approved video and post details to n8n; configure your platform credentials and upload steps in that workflow. Social platform API permissions and limits still apply.

When you approve publishing, the video is sent to the platform or your n8n workflow. Nothing is posted before approval.

Where your files are saved

FramePilot saves clips, source videos, settings and the downloaded speech model in your local application data folder. You can change the storage location in Settings → Storage. Keep enough free disk space for your videos and clips.

  • Windows: %LOCALAPPDATA%\FramePilot
  • macOS: ~/Library/Application Support/FramePilot
  • Linux: ~/.local/share/FramePilot

Need help?

  • Port 8765 is in use: close the other program using that port and restart FramePilot.
  • Speech model did not download: check your internet connection and available disk space, then restart the app.
  • Linux cannot open a window: install GTK 3 and WebKitGTK 4.1 from your distribution's package manager.
  • Account connection does not finish: finish signing in in your system browser, then return to FramePilot.
